baml experiments

We co-design BAML with the agents and people who write it. That means running experiments instead of arguing from taste. Put models in front of the language and watch what they actually do; when the data disagrees with us, the language changes. This page collects the runs, with receipts.

Can models learn BAML from describe alone?
6 models · 0 compiled · $9.55 reserved

Models built the same small BAML project with no syntax guide. Their only teacher was baml describe; compilation remained the hard gate.

 Tool calling for gpt-realtime: baml vs native
19/19 vs 17/19 · live sessions · three suites

Two identical voice sessions, one difference: who turns your words into a tool call. The native lane uses JSON-schema tools in the realtime session. The baml lane delegates to a typed BAML function. The two failures tell you more than the seventeen passes.

Data lives in the experiments Convex project. Graders, harnesses, and raw runs are in the repo under tools/experiments/ and tools/baml-bench/.
