# Types

Creating types is a key part of integrating LLMs with apps.

Types give structure to your prompts, bring LLM responses in
line with your UI and database, and allow you to specify
the behavior of agents.

The quality of the types in an app built around LLMs is the
most important factor in whether the app works as expected.
So BAML gives you a lot of features for crafting types and
provides strong guarantees that responses will conform to your
output types, no matter what model you use.

So let's get good at types.
