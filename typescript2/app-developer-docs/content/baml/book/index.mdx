## Before you begin

These chapters assume familiarity with functions, classes, unions, pattern matching, arrays, and lambdas. Interfaces builds on the error effects introduced in [Handle errors](/baml/book/errors); [Run work concurrently](/baml/book/concurrency) uses those effects to explain futures.

BAML examples are excerpts from complete compiler-checked projects. Examples that intentionally fail are checked for their expected diagnostics. Inferred signatures and diagnostic output are labeled separately from executable source.
